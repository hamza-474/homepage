Projects

Here are some of the projects I have worked on, from AI automation and data analysis to my Harvard CS50x coursework. Each one taught me something practical about building software people can actually use.

FinLedger AI

AI Automation Python

FinLedger AI is a personal finance assistant that reads bank statements and receipts, categorises spending, and produces a clear monthly summary. It replaces manual spreadsheet work with an automated pipeline, so a month of transactions can be reviewed in minutes instead of hours.

What it does: imports transactions, classifies them, flags unusual spending, and reports totals by category.

Data Analysis Projects

Python SQL

I have built several data analysis projects with Python and SQL, including World Cup tournament simulations and database queries over songs and movies datasets in CS50. The work covers cleaning raw data, writing precise queries, and presenting results in plain language with charts and summaries.

What it does: turns raw datasets into answers, such as which teams are most likely to win, or how listening habits change over time.
